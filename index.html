FlockTrot

Plan group camping trips together, all in one app.

FlockTrot is a mobile app for planning group camping trips with family and friends. It helps a group build a shared itinerary, coordinate who's bringing which supplies and meals, track shared costs and settle up, and keep everyone's campsite details in one place — instead of juggling group texts and spreadsheets.
