Delete Your FlockTrot Account

Effective date: August 4, 2026

The fastest way to delete your account is right in the app: open the menu, go to Camper Profile, and tap Delete Account — it deletes your account immediately, no waiting required.

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion here instead. This applies whether or not you still have the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to flocktrot@gmail.com from the email address associated with your FlockTrot account.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account," and let us know the email address to confirm it's you.
  3. We will confirm your identity, then delete your account and associated data as described below, normally within 7 days.
Email us to delete your account

What gets deleted

When you request deletion, we delete:

  • Your account and profile (name, email, Google account link)
  • Your group membership and group claims
  • Trips, groups, and campsite maps you personally created, where you are the sole owner
  • Your itinerary events, supply items, expenses, and gear list entries

What may be retained

Some data may be retained after your account is deleted:

  • Shared trip content: if you're a member of a trip or group that other people still use (for example, a shared itinerary or expense log), your individual contributions to that shared content may remain visible to the remaining group so the trip stays usable for other members — your personal account and profile link to it is removed.
  • Legal/security records: we may retain limited records where required by law, or to prevent fraud or abuse, for a reasonable period after deletion.

Questions

Contact us at flocktrot@gmail.com with any questions about this process or our Privacy Policy.
